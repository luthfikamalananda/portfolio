# Portfolio

A single-page personal portfolio for a software engineer (currently frontend-focused), meant to be professional yet playful rather than corporate.

## Language

### People & identity

**Owner**:
The person the portfolio is about; the one whose name, skills and work are shown.
_Avoid_: User, me, author

**Visitor**:
Anyone reading the portfolio, typically a recruiter, client or fellow engineer.
_Avoid_: User, reader, guest

### Page structure

**Section**:
One named, anchorable block of the single page (e.g. Hero, About, Projects, Contact).
_Avoid_: Page, screen, block

**Status**:
A short, current-tense line about the Owner's availability and focus right now (e.g. "Open to work · Jakarta · Learning Rust").
_Avoid_: Currently, now, bio

**Beyond Code**:
The Section about the Owner off the clock — the fighting-game community, story games, streaming, and staying active. Opens with the Player Card, then the Modes.
_Avoid_: Fun facts, hobbies, personal

**Player Card**:
The Owner presented as a fighting-game character: photo, main character, rank, joke stats and a special move.
_Avoid_: Profile card, gamer card

**Mode**:
One panel of Beyond Code, named like a game menu entry: Versus (the Tekken community), Story Mode (story games played), Stream (YouTube) or Training (gym and sports).
_Avoid_: Tab, category

**Community**:
The fighting-game community the Owner belongs to and helps run (as committee member and caster).
_Avoid_: Club, group, team

**Resume**:
The downloadable document summarising the Owner's career; exists in one version per Locale, and the Visitor chooses which version on download.
_Avoid_: CV (in UI copy, pick one)

### Career

**Journey**:
The single chronological story of the Owner's career, mixing education, work and organisation experience.
_Avoid_: Timeline, experience, history, education section

**Milestone**:
One entry in the Journey, tagged by kind: Education, Work or Organization.
_Avoid_: Entry, item, event, job

**Latest Education**:
The most recent Education Milestone, also surfaced on its own in About.
_Avoid_: Last degree, pendidikan terakhir

### Work & skills

**Project**:
A piece of work the Owner built and shows off, with a role, a stack, a year and outbound links.
_Avoid_: Work, portfolio item, case

**Featured Project**:
One of the few Projects (three) given a large card and an image; all others are shown compactly.
_Avoid_: Highlight, pinned, main project

**Skill**:
A named technology or ability the Owner uses, belonging to exactly one Skill Group. Never carries a percentage or level.
_Avoid_: Tech, tool, competency

**Skill Group**:
A category of Skills (Languages, Frameworks, Tools, Currently Learning).
_Avoid_: Category, stack

### Presentation

**Locale**:
The language the portfolio's copy is shown in; English by default, Indonesian on toggle.
_Avoid_: Language, translation
